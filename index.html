Meet Ashayla.

Ashayla Lewis is the founder and lead stylist of Annu Beauty, an Atlanta studio built on a simple belief: healthy hair and honest education go hand in hand. Every retwist, style, and consultation is rooted in the same standard — careful technique, genuine care, and a commitment to helping clients understand their own hair, not just leave with a fresh look.

Outside the chair, she's building the guides, courses, and community that carry that same care beyond the studio doors.
