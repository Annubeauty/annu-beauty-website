Why a certified Sisterlocks consultant matters

Hey, Beauty. Sisterlocks is a trademarked method with its own tools, technique, and training. Only consultants trained and certified by Sisterlocks can install real Sisterlocks. Anyone else who offers them is doing something different under a borrowed name.

I've seen too many women in Atlanta get overcharged, rushed, or left with damaged hair because nobody taught them what to look for. Here are the red flags:

  • They aren't listed in the official Sisterlocks consultant registry. I'm listed there as Ashayla Lewis.
  • They skip the consultation and quote a price without looking at your scalp, density, and strands.
  • They can't explain the early stages honestly: slippage, bunching, and the time it takes for locs to settle.

My mother, Kia Lewis, has practiced Sisterlocks for more than 20 years. I got my first two sets from her hands, and she trained me before I turned 18. That's two generations of care for thick, kinky, beautiful hair. And it's why my mission is simple: to help Black women feel confident in their hair and in their Sisterlocks, Microlocks, or loc journey.

Sisterlocks or Microlocks?

Both are small, uniform locs that give you freedom from the restyling cycle. The difference is the method: real Sisterlocks use the official Sisterlocks technique and can only be installed by a certified consultant. Microlocks are a similar style done with a different technique, starting at $1,000 at Annu Beauty. Not sure which fits your hair? That's exactly what the consultation is for. Sisterlocks questions, answered

How much do Sisterlocks cost in Atlanta?

At Annu Beauty, a Sisterlocks install with Ashayla starts at $1,300, and Sisterlocks extensions start at $3,000. Every install starts with a $75 consultation that goes toward your install. Your final price depends on your length, density, and loc size.

Is Ashayla a certified Sisterlocks consultant?

Yes. Ashayla Lewis has been certified since age 18 and is listed in the official Sisterlocks consultant registry for Georgia. She was trained by her mother, Kia Lewis, who has practiced Sisterlocks for more than 20 years.

How long does a Sisterlocks install take?

It depends on your hair length, density, and the loc size you choose. You'll get a time estimate at your consultation so you can plan ahead.

How often do Sisterlocks need retightening?

Every 3–5 weeks is ideal. Retightening is priced by how many weeks it's been since your last visit, so staying on schedule keeps your locs healthy and your cost lower. See retightening prices.

Can I get Sisterlocks if my hair is thin or damaged?

Many women start Sisterlocks to give their hair a break from damage and strain. Every case is different, so we check your scalp and strands at the consultation and tell you honestly whether now is the right time.

I already have Sisterlocks. Can I transfer to Annu Beauty?

Yes. Book a $45 transfer consultation, in person or virtual. We'll look at your locs, your scalp, and your retightening pattern, then make a plan to keep your crown healthy.
