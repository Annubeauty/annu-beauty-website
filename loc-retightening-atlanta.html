Why booking on time matters

Hey, Beauty. Your hair keeps growing whether you book or not. The longer you wait, the more new growth there is to work through, so your appointment takes longer, costs more, and puts more tension on your roots. Staying on a 3–5 week schedule keeps your locs neat, your scalp happy, and your cost at its lowest.

Every retightening is also a check-in. We look at your scalp, your parts, and any thinning or weak spots, and we talk through what your hair needs between visits. That's the education part of every Annu Beauty experience.

Retightening questions, answered

How much does loc retightening cost in Atlanta?

At Annu Beauty, retightening starts at $165 with Shushonaw or Danielle and $200 with Ashayla when you come back within 3–5 weeks. The price goes up the longer it's been since your last visit, up to $425 at 11+ weeks.

How often should Sisterlocks or Microlocks be retightened?

Every 3–5 weeks is ideal for most clients. It keeps your new growth easy to work with, your locs neat, and your price at its lowest.

Do you retighten locs installed by another loctician?

Yes. Book a $45 transfer consultation first, in person or virtual. We'll look at how your locs were installed and maintained, then build your plan from there.

Who retightens Sisterlocks at Annu Beauty?

Ashayla Lewis, a certified Sisterlocks consultant listed in the official Sisterlocks registry. Microlocks and loc maintenance are also available with Shushonaw and Danielle.

I'm way overdue. Can I still book?

Yes, and no judgment. Book your retightening at the 11+ week rate, and we'll make a plan to get you back on a healthy schedule.

What hours are you open?

Annu Beauty is open daily, 9 AM to 7 PM, by appointment. Book online anytime.
