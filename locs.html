Know the difference

Sisterlocks or Microlocks?

Sisterlocks®

Starting at $1,300

Very fine, uniform locs made with the trademarked Sisterlocks method and tool. Only a certified consultant can install them. Curl them, pin them up, style them like loose hair.

Microlocks

Starting at $1,000

Small, uniform locs done with a different technique, with more flexibility in loc size and a lower starting investment.

Not sure which one is right for you?

That's exactly what the consultation is for. Want length now? Ask about loc extensions, starting at $3,000 with hair included.

Before you come in

How to wear your hair to your consult.

Your consultation is where I see your real hair, so please come with:

  • Your hair clean and fully dry
  • Your hair out and loose: no braids, twists, wig, weave or extensions
  • No heavy oils, gels or leave-ins on the day
  • Your hair in its natural state (no need to press or blow it out)
  • Your questions, and photos of loc looks you love
