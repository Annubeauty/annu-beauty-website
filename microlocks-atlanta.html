Microlocks or Sisterlocks?

Both are small, uniform locs that free you from the restyling cycle. Sisterlocks use the trademarked Sisterlocks method and can only be installed by a certified consultant like Ashayla. Microlocks are a similar small loc done with a different technique, with a lower starting investment and more flexibility in sizing. Not sure which fits your hair and lifestyle? Microlocks questions, answered

How much do Microlocks cost in Atlanta?

At Annu Beauty, Microlocks start at $1,000 for the first 5 inches with Shushonaw or Danielle, plus $100 for each extra inch, or $1,250 for the first 5 inches with Ashayla. Microlocks with extensions start at $3,000. Every install starts with a $75 consultation that goes toward your install.

What's the difference between Microlocks and Sisterlocks?

Sisterlocks use the trademarked Sisterlocks method and must be installed by a certified consultant. Microlocks are a similar small loc done with a different technique, with a lower starting price and more flexibility in sizing.

How long does a Microlocks install take?

It depends on your hair length, density, and the loc size you choose. You'll get a time estimate at your consultation so you can plan ahead.

How often do Microlocks need retightening?

Every 3–5 weeks is ideal. Retightening starts at $165 and is priced by how many weeks it's been since your last visit.

Can I get Microlocks with extensions?

Yes. Microlocks extensions start at $3,000 and include 6 inches of extension hair, a free 3-week follow-up, and a complimentary retightening.

Who installs Microlocks at Annu Beauty?

Ashayla Lewis, Shushonaw Shiloh, and Danielle Ford all install Microlocks. Pricing depends on your stylist, and your consultation helps you choose.
